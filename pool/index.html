A letter

Dear Cardano,

BEACN became a stake pool in November 2025: one operator in Chicago, bare metal in a home rack, zero margin, and one idea — point a light at the parts of Cardano nobody looks at.

It grew into much more than a pool. We followed genesis ADA to the present and graded every link by its evidence. We cast 59 governance votes in the open. We wrote the Bible, the Bitcoin whitepaper and Cardano's Constitution onto the chain page by page, and built a Library to read them back. We built a studio so anyone could make art that lives entirely on Cardano, and we minted 507 pieces of our own: games, music, satire, films and, at the very end, a goodbye.

We made one block. We never got big. That was never the point. There was no team behind it: just one person and the AI agents working beside him. And what we made is still here.

The pool retires at epoch 656 and our servers are going dark. The scrolls and the art stay on Cardano. The code, the data, the films and a reading copy of every scroll stay on this page, free to read, check and build on.

To every delegator who trusted us with their stake, and to everyone who read a scroll, minted something, checked our work or argued with a vote: thank you for keeping the light with us.

For builders

Check it yourself.

BEACN’s Koios mirror went dark with the servers, but the scrolls never lived there. Every one can still be rebuilt straight from Cardano through the free public Koios API — no key, no account, nothing of ours — and checked against its on-chain hash.

From a terminal — public Koios, nothing to host

Python 3 and git. One small download gives you a menu of every scroll and mint, and every command here was run against the public API and matched the chain.

1 · Get the lookup tool and reader (about 1 MB)
git clone --depth 1 --filter=blob:none --sparse https://github.com/BEACNpool/ledger-scrolls
cd ledger-scrolls && git sparse-checkout set archive koios-viewer viewers registry
2 · Pick anything from a menu
python3 archive/lookup.py
3 · Fetch a mint's art (nothing else to install)
python3 archive/lookup.py get beacn-sigil
4 · Rebuild a scroll (installs the reader once)
cd koios-viewer && python3 -m venv .venv && . .venv/bin/activate && pip install -e . && cd ..
python3 archive/lookup.py get bible

In a browser — bring your own free mirror

Public Koios won’t answer web pages directly, so a browser needs a tiny CORS mirror. Ours is gone; yours takes about two minutes and costs nothing.

  1. Make a free Cloudflare account and create a Worker. No domain is needed.
  2. Paste worker.js and deploy. Your source is https://<name>.<account>.workers.dev/api/v1.
  3. Open the Library, find Your trust anchors, paste it under Data source and press Set.
  4. Every scroll is checked against its on-chain SHA-256 before it shows, so a mirror can refuse you but can’t fool you.
  • Every scroll, from a terminal. All 22 scrolls in the Library catalog rebuild through public Koios with the open readers, matching the hash the chain records wherever it records one.
  • The Library in a browser, with your own mirror. Tested with the open recipe: the registry, the Bitcoin whitepaper, Hosky and the spec all loaded and verified.
  • Everything else on this page. The preserved mint art, films, reading copies, explorer and relay snapshots, match scorecard and DRep archive are served by GitHub Pages. They do not depend on BEACN’s former servers. Interactive chain operations retain their own service requirements.
  • Ledger Chess, Mint a Scroll, Ledger Book and the Neon Door still call BEACN’s retired mirror and stop at the chain step. Their code is open, so anyone can point it at their own mirror.
  • koios.beacn.workers.dev BEACN’s hosted mirror, retired with the servers.

How faithful this one is

The hardest of the three. The bird is constructed (middle finger up, knuckles on the fist), not traced; temple hair, clipped pupils, clean hat. The silhouettes were traced from the colour regions of the original and simplified until they fit the budget; the linework was rebuilt. Held beside the original at full size the differences are visible — that is the honest trade for a picture that never needs anything else to survive.
